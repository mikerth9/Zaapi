Q: Do I need to separate my clothes?
A: No. We sort everything by colour and fabric at the facility. You can use any bag.

Q: Will my clothes be washed with other people's?
A: No. Every order is washed separately.

Q: Do you use eco-friendly products?
A: Yes. Our detergents are biodegradable and phosphate-free, and dry cleaning uses solvent-free wet cleaning where possible.

Q: Can you remove this stain?
A: Send us a photo on LINE and we'll tell you how likely it is to come out. Fresh stains (under 24 hours) come out far more often.

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. We send LINE updates at pickup, when your items reach the facility, when they're ready, and when they're out for delivery. You can also type "status" in the LINE chat.

Q: How do you weigh Wash & Fold?
A: Dry, before washing, on a calibrated scale. The bill includes a photo of the scale.

Q: Can I ask for a specific driver or time?
A: We can't promise a driver, but you can choose any 2-hour slot from 08:00 to 21:00.

Q: Can I tip the driver?
A: Tips aren't expected. If you'd like to tip, it goes 100% to the driver.

Q: Do you wash pet bedding?
A: Yes, in a separate machine, for 200 baht per item. Please shake off loose hair first.

Q: What if I'm not home at delivery?
A: We can leave items with building reception, a neighbour you choose, or at a locker. Tell us beforehand.

Q: Can I cancel an order?
A: Free before pickup. After pickup, you pay for any work already started plus the delivery fee.
