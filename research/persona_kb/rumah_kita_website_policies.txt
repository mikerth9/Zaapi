Rumah Kita — Shipping & Returns

Shipping
Flat-rate shipping: RM8 to West Malaysia, RM15 to East Malaysia (Sabah, Sarawak, Labuan).
Free shipping on orders over RM150 to West Malaysia.
Bulky items may incur additional charges.
Orders ship within 1-2 working days.

Returns & Refunds
We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.
Damaged or defective items: report within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and parcel. We will replace or refund at no cost.
Customers in East Malaysia pay return shipping unless the item arrived damaged.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days of receiving the return.

Marketplace orders
Orders placed on Shopee or Lazada are handled under that marketplace's policies. Please contact us through the marketplace chat.
