# Appendix C — Integration guides checked against the providers' own docs

*All 50 integration articles on help.zaapi.com (from `help.zaapi.com/llms.txt`), read on 25 Sep 2026, compared with each provider's current developer or help documentation, read the same day. Tags as in Appendix A, plus `[provider]` = the provider's own documentation. Where I could only check a claim against our earlier research (`zaapi_ai_agent_activation_research.md`), or couldn't check it without a seller login, I say so.*

## The short version

- **Clearly wrong or out of date:** 9 claims, across WhatsApp, Instagram and LINE. The worst is the WhatsApp pricing page: it tells merchants that replies inside the 24-hour window stop being free after 1,000 a month, and "became chargeable on 1 October 2026". Meta says all non-template replies are free, and it has announced no such change. A merchant who reads this will expect to pay Meta for every AI reply on WhatsApp, on top of Zaapi's AI tokens.
- **Contradicts itself:** 4 places (template approval time; Meta routing settings; the WhatsApp "connect" section; the name of the go-live node).
- **Leaves out things that stop the agent working:** marketplace connections expire (Shopee after a year, Lazada after six months) with no warning in the guides, and the LINE guide doesn't tell merchants to turn off LINE's own auto-replies, which can reply alongside the AI.
- **Throws away history the AI could learn from:** the WhatsApp guide tells merchants to decline Meta's offer to share up to 180 days of chats. Gmail imports 7 days. LINE history needs a paid add-on (฿555 a month), and nothing from before 1 Jan 2024 can be downloaded. This is direct evidence for the "draft from history" change in the memo.
- **Your LINE point:** I couldn't confirm it from LINE's public docs; see below.

## Your LINE point: what LINE's docs say today

You noted that the LINE guide is out of date because the credentials can no longer be taken from the developer site. LINE's current documentation doesn't support that exact point:

- Since **4 Sep 2024**, a Messaging API channel can't be created in the LINE Developers Console. It's created by enabling the Messaging API in LINE Official Account Manager. **Zaapi's Step 1 already does it this way.**
- The **long-lived channel access token** "can be issued at any time from the Messaging API tab" of the LINE Developers Console, with no deprecation notice. That's Zaapi's Step 2.
- The only Developers Console removal since then is the sent-message count in the Statistics tab (26 Nov 2025). Zaapi's guide doesn't use it.

So either your account shows something the public docs don't (different menus for Thai accounts, or the Business Manager link-up; LINE made that mandatory for new accounts in Japan only), or the problem is in a different step. A screenshot of what you saw would settle it. The LINE items that are definitely wrong are #13, #14, #16 and #17.

## Themes

1. **Nothing checks the guides against the providers.** Three platforms have changed underneath them: Meta retired the Handover Protocol, Meta changed WhatsApp pricing (July 2025), and LINE reply tokens now last one minute. The WhatsApp pricing page has also been rewritten with a date that hasn't happened yet.
2. **Connections expire silently.** Shopee after 365 days and Lazada after six months. For an agent that has to *stay* live, that's a scheduled switch-off nobody warns the merchant about. It belongs with the brief's "still live at 30 days" data.
3. **History is available but thrown away.** WhatsApp coexistence offers 180 days (Zaapi says decline it), LINE offers history for ฿555 a month, and Gmail imports 7 days. Shopee, Lazada, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram all import 90 days automatically. This is the raw material for drafting knowledge and scenarios from history.
4. **Costs are unclear.** WhatsApp (wrong), LINE (wrong window) and AI tokens (separate) together make an AI launch look more expensive and less predictable than it is. That feeds H14.

## Addendum (28 Sep 2026): history that setup could use

- **WhatsApp (coexistence):** Meta lets a business share up to **180 days** of one-to-one chats (not groups) when it connects. The business must approve during connection, and declining is final unless it disconnects and reconnects. The partner must start the sync **within 24 hours**, or the business has to redo the flow. History arrives by webhook in three phases (day 0–1, 1–90, 90–180). Zaapi's guide tells merchants to decline because import "is not yet supported" `[provider]`. **Memo:** accept it at connection, in weeks 2–6.
- **LINE:** the Messaging API can't fetch chats from before connection. The only route is the OA Chat package export (฿555 a month plus VAT, charged as a full month; history before 1 Jan 2024 can't be downloaded) `[provider]`. **Memo:** chats arriving since connection are used by default. A one-month package export is an optional uplift, included in the AI Success Kit.
